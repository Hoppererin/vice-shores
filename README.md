An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: drive, steal cars, dodge the Vice Shores PD, and run four jobs for Mama Lou across a living coastal city — all playable offline in a desktop browser from static files.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD / arrows to move and drive, SHIFT to run, E to enter or exit a car, F to punch, P to pause. Progress saves to your browser automatically.

## The game

You are Rico Vega, a driver-for-hire back in Vice Shores. The city is a 3200×2400 open world with a road grid, traffic, pedestrians, a beach and ocean, a park and a plaza, five districts, and a wanted system that brings the police after you — up to four patrol cars and foot cops who will grab you if they get close.

Four jobs from Mama Lou: a first fare in a yellow taxi, a timed package run from the Marina docks, a getaway where you start with two wanted stars, and a final job stealing a white sports car. Finish all four and the city is yours in free roam.

Everything is original and self-contained: vanilla canvas rendering, synthesized WebAudio sound, no image, font or audio files, and no network requests.
